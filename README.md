## Task 1 — Digital Sports Ground Booking Platform

### Problem and Investigation

Informal conversations with 10 students between 21 and 24 October 2026 indicated that students generally visit the sports ground and play if it is available, or wait or join others when it is occupied. The investigation identified an opportunity for a more structured way to request and coordinate ground usage.

A student who heads the sports club also indicated that a digital platform could be useful. This was informal feedback, not formal institutional approval.

### Solution

A Streamlit-based minimum viable product (MVP) was developed and deployed to explore a digital sports-ground booking workflow.

**Deployment:** [Open the live application](https://ground-booking.streamlit.app/)

The current booking-duration rule is a minimum of 30 minutes and a maximum of 60 minutes. Eligibility and authorization policies require confirmation from the relevant institution.

The Task 1 folder contains the report and implementation artifacts, including application source, dependencies, and test-case documentation where included. Deployment alone does not establish that every feature works correctly. Test results and hypothetical scenarios must be distinguished accurately.

## Task 2 — Curriculum and Lab Audit

### Objective

Examine the supplied curriculum information, compare selected industry job requirements, identify potential practical-learning gaps, and propose improvements to course content and assessment.

### Scope

The audit covers curriculum mapping, selected software engineering and AI/ML job postings, practical learning activities, proposed curriculum adjustments, AI-assisted learning exercises, and supporting references.

The proposed redesign is a recommendation rather than an approved institutional curriculum change. Any discrepancy between the supplied course-credit rows and the stated credit baseline is documented as an unresolved issue requiring verification against authoritative curriculum records.

The Task 2 folder contains the report and supporting materials available in the repository, such as curriculum maps, redesign proposals, job-posting analysis, AI exercises, validation notes, references, and an interactive view where included.

## Limitations

- Task 1 uses informal conversations with 10 students; the findings are not a statistically representative campus-wide survey.
- The deployed MVP is a prototype. Deployment does not verify every feature, concurrent booking behavior, failure condition, or edge case.
- Booking eligibility and institutional authorization policies require confirmation unless supported by separate approval evidence.
- Hypothetical test walkthroughs are not equivalent to executed tests.
- The supplied curriculum map may not exactly match the latest official programme structure or the curriculum for a particular student batch.
- Any discrepancy in curriculum credit totals requires reconciliation against authoritative records.
- Selected job postings illustrate specific employer requirements rather than the entire job market.
- Proposed curriculum changes require academic review and approval.
- Manual checks and informal feedback do not replace systematic testing, faculty validation, or employer evaluation.
